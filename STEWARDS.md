[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#roles)). Where no steward is listed, DIDAS as
swiyu orchestrator holds provisional stewardship. Where a sector has no council
yet, its working group's position stands in.

Escalations go to the swiyu ecosystem Steering Circle, which DIDAS reaches as
swiyu orchestrator.

To propose a steward or record a council, open a pull request that changes this
file, with a changelog entry.
