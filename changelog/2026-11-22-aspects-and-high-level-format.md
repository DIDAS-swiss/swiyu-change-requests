# 2026-11-22: Aspects and high-level format

- Change requests are inputs to a pipeline. They describe the obstacle, the
  actors affected and the requested outcome and leave the solution to the
  swiyu team. The template is reduced accordingly.
- `ASPECTS.md` proposes eleven aspects for sorting change requests: costs for
  actors, constraints of use, technological hurdles, organisational hurdles,
  governance and trust framework, technological improvements, defects,
  infrastructure, law and ordinances, interoperability, documentation. Status:
  proposal for the consultation.
- One intake form, organised by aspect, replaces the separate change request
  and feasibility concern forms. New labels `aspect: <id>`.
- README, contributing guide and governance rewritten in plainer language.
- The README, governance and contributing guide state that the whole concept
  is a proposal for discussion; the website shows this in the footer of every
  page.
